Anilife records redacted diagnostics locally. On official builds, a one-time privacy notice appears before automatic upload can be scheduled. You can turn automatic uploads off at any time in **Settings → App**.

## Export a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open App settings">
    Open **Settings → App** and select **Export diagnostics**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the problem">
    Add what you were doing, what you expected, and when the problem happened. A screenshot is optional and is never captured automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or share the ZIP">
    Review the destination and share the report only with a person or service you trust.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Reports can include app and device configuration, Android version, performance samples, playback and buffering statistics, provider hosts, network timing, work status, app logs, crashes, and native exit information exposed by Android.

Anilife redacts passwords, cookies, authorisation headers, account tokens, email addresses, signed media URLs, anime titles, slugs, and other sensitive content identifiers before creating the ZIP. A screenshot can still contain anything visible in the image, so attach only one you intend to send.

## Choose the local detail level

Open **Settings → App → Diagnostic detail**:

- **Standard** is the default. It keeps failures and compact operation summaries while sampling repetitive successful events.
- **Enhanced** keeps more allowlisted event detail until you switch back to Standard. Use it only while reproducing a difficult problem.

Changing detail does not enable uploads. Reports identify the active detail mode and include sampled or dropped-event counters so maintainers can tell when a trace is incomplete.

## Uploads

- **Upload diagnostics now** sends one redacted report to the private Anilife diagnostics service.
- **Automatically upload diagnostics** sends at most one report per day. It is enabled after the one-time notice only when you have not already made a choice.
- The first automatic report contains only events recorded after automatic upload was enabled. Nothing is scheduled before you acknowledge the notice.
- Accepted uploads are private and retained for 30 days.

Turning automatic upload off cancels queued work and removes staged reports. An existing explicit on or off choice is preserved when you update Anilife. Local export remains available without enabling uploads.
